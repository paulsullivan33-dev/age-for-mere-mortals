Hash the original, then hash a decryption streamed through stdout:
$ sha256sum note.txt
d3493ffe3993c141bfe107942846a7bacfafe1c1cd429347dcbdab1bb89dc268  note.txt
$ age -d -i alice-key.txt note.txt.age | sha256sum
d3493ffe3993c141bfe107942846a7bacfafe1c1cd429347dcbdab1bb89dc268  -

Encrypt straight from a pipe, no plaintext file on disk:
$ tar -cf - rota.txt | age -r <alice> -o rota.tar.age
[exit 0]
$ age -d -i alice-key.txt rota.tar.age | tar -tvf -
